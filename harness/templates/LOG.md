next session (human or agent) can start in one minute instead of ten.

- **Done:** requirements finished (IDs), commits
- **State of tests:** `gw.sh check` result
- **Decisions:** anything the GDD does not say yet
- **Blocked / questions for the human:**
- **Next step:** the single next thing to do
