# Colony Sim

> A tiny colony builder where you balance food, wood and housing to grow a
> settlement, but every new mouth needs feeding.

## The spark
The smallest possible economy that still has a decision: produce food,
produce materials, or expand housing.

## Core loop
Every tick (0.5 s) buildings produce, people eat, the colony grows or starves.
The player builds farms, lumber mills and houses.

## Not wanted
Combat, maps, multiplayer, save/load. This is a harness demo.
